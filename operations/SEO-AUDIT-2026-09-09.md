## Competitive position and content decisions

[College Pulse](https://collegepulse.com/) positions around a large college student research panel. [User Interviews](https://www.userinterviews.com/usability-tests) explicitly addresses recruiting usability test participants. [UXArmy](https://uxarmy.com/participant-recruitment/) describes managed recruitment. These primary pages demonstrate category language and buyer questions; no backlink authority, keyword difficulty, or competitor conversion data was available.

Campus Takes should win suitable small studies with clear feasibility, direct service, careful screening and transparent evidence. It should not imply the scale, national representativeness or geographic coverage of a larger provider. Added format-specific descriptions and buying answers on one strong service page rather than several thin overlapping landing pages.

The next valuable content is a real completed client case study with permission: audience, screening approach, invited/qualified/completed counts, actual timeframe, incentive and limitations. The existing July report is useful evidence, but n=20 is not the current total panel or a guarantee of future performance.
