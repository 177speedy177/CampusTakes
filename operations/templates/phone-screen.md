# Five-minute applicant screen

Use only when a human check is warranted. Ask the same core questions in the same
order; do not coach the applicant toward approval.

1. “What school are you currently enrolled at, and what program are you studying?”
2. “What academic level are you in, and when do you expect to graduate?”
3. “In your own words, what do you think Campus Takes is?”
4. “Have you done paid interviews, usability tests, focus groups, or surveys before?
   About how often?”
5. “If a study is recorded, what information would you want before deciding?”
6. “Is the phone number and school email on your application the best way to reach you?”

Pass when the applicant is understandable, answers consistently with the application,
understands voluntary research participation, and shows no material identity or fraud
concern. Do not score accent, polish, confidence, or similarity to the reviewer.

Record only the decision, material inconsistencies, verification method, date, and
reviewer. Do not write subjective personality judgments.
