Before you apply

A few things to know

What do I actually do in a study?

You might try a physical product, take part in an academic study, join an interview or focus group, complete a survey, or test a digital service. Academic studies may involve tasks, questionnaires, or discussions. Your invitation explains the specific activities before you decide.

How much will I earn, and how am I paid?

Pay varies by study. Before you agree to take part, you will see the payment amount, method, and timing. Payment is for completing a study; joining the panel or answering screening questions is unpaid.

When will I get my first invitation?

There is no set wait time. Each study calls for different backgrounds, experiences, or interests. We contact you when your profile could fit a study, and you may need to answer a few more questions to qualify.

Do I have to accept every study?

No. Choose the studies that fit your schedule. If you book a session, please attend or let us know as early as possible if your plans change.

Who can join?

You must be 18 or older and currently enrolled at a U.S. college or university. The application requires a school email ending in .edu and a phone number. We may ask for more information to confirm eligibility.
