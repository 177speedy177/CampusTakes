Methodology

Single-wave survey fielded July 27 to 28, 2026. The study was capped at 20 completed responses. Fielding opened when invitations went out and closed when the quota filled, 24 hours later. Panelists verify enrollment with a school email address at signup, and each response links to one verified panelist record. Percentages are shares of the 20 completed responses and may not sum to 100 due to rounding. Payout timing is the median of the 10 payouts issued through July 28, 2026. Panelists are capped at two studies per month with a 90-day cooldown between studies in the same category. Open answers are published with written consent.
