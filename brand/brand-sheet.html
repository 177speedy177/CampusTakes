Color

Navy and white. No third color.

There is no accent hue in this system. When something needs to stand out, use a lighter shade of the same navy. Adding a contrasting color is the fastest way to make something look off-brand.

icon-white.pngProfile picture on dark platforms.

Rules

  • Keep clear space of at least one bubble height on every side.
  • Lock the aspect ratio when resizing. The mark is 1.23 : 1.
  • Use the white version on anything dark, including photos.
  • Below about 120px wide, drop the wordmark and use the mark alone.
  • Do not stretch, squash, or rotate it.
  • Do not add a shadow, glow, outline, or gradient.
  • Do not recolor it to anything but navy or white.
  • Do not rebuild the wordmark in another font.
Voice

How Campus Takes writes

RuleWhy
Plain-spokenSay the thing. No "elevate," "seamless," "unleash," or "game-changing."
Numbers carry the argumentCite the count next to the percentage. At small samples, a percentage alone overstates precision.
Claim only what happenedDescribe what we do. Do not promise how well it works until it has worked.
No em dashesUse a comma, a colon, or a full stop.
No borrowed credibilityNo client logos, testimonials, or case studies until they are real and permitted.
No institution countsNever state a number of campuses, colleges, or schools. Say "a US-based research panel of college students."
