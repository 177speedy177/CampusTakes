Why Campus Takes

Recruitment that fits your team

Need us to manage the recruit? We can handle screening, scheduling, reminders, and incentive coordination. Already have a recruiting team? We can source students and hand over at the point you choose.

Screened against your criteria

For interviews and usability tests, we phone-screen shortlisted students using your approved questions before booking. You receive the screening outcomes and any details still needing confirmation.

Invitations sent to matched students

We invite panel members whose profiles match your audience, then check study eligibility with your screener. Study links are shared directly with those students.

Useful project records

Follow each participant from screening through scheduling and completion. We agree on reporting, handoffs, and payment responsibilities before recruiting begins.

Limits on repeat participation

Our panel policy allows up to two studies per month, with 90 days between studies in the same category. We check participation recorded through Campus Takes before booking.

Before you book

Recruiting questions

What does student participant recruitment cost?

We quote each study after checking the audience, participant count, session length, screening needs, and timeline. Your written quote includes the recruiting scope and participant incentives before work starts. Send your study brief for a quote.

How quickly can you recruit participants?

We build a recruiting schedule around your audience, quotas and session dates. Share your brief and we will confirm the delivery timeline before work begins.

How do you verify students?

Applicants confirm control of their school email and phone with one-time codes. Automated checks assess eligibility and consistency, with human review for exceptions. Current enrollment is self-reported unless independently checked. Tell us if your study requires independent enrollment evidence so we can include it in screening.

Can I target a particular school, major, or product user?

Yes. We specialize in recruiting students attending Penn State and offer targeted recruitment across U.S. campuses. Include your target schools, majors, product experience and quotas in the brief. We assess availability for your audience and use your approved screener to confirm study-specific fit before booking.

Does Campus Takes moderate or analyze the research?

Your team designs, moderates, and analyzes the study. Campus Takes handles participant recruitment, screening, scheduling, reminders, attendance tracking, and incentive coordination.

Can students join the panel here?

Yes. Apply on our student page. Each invitation explains the time commitment and incentive, and participation is optional.
