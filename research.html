Why Campus Takes

13 of 20 respondents had never been paid for research

That is what our first panel study measured. The full breakdown, the method, and the limits are in the Panel Report.

Verified, not self-reported

Every panelist signs up with a verified school email, and every response ties back to one panelist record.

Hand-screened, not crowdsourced

A person reviews every application and screens participants against the criteria you set for your study. No anonymous pool to draw from.

Small on purpose

A marketplace sells access to a pool. We keep the panel small enough to know who is in it, and we screen every participant by hand.

Capped participation

Two studies per panelist per month, and a 90-day cooldown between studies in the same category. Nobody here answers everything.

How it works

From screener to scheduled sessions

1

Tell us who you need

Share your quotas, screener, and format: moderated interviews, unmoderated usability tests, or surveys. We tell you what is feasible, and what is not, before you commit.

2

We recruit and verify

We match enrollment-verified students to your criteria, screen them against your questions, and confirm every slot.

3

You run the research

You choose the format and the tool for the session. We confirm participants, send reminders, and pay them afterwards. You pay only for the sessions that are actually completed.
